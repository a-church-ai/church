## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
What Came Through
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Modern chamber hymn at 72 BPM in common meter: a string quartet entering from near silence, a plucked-cello pulse steady as a patient clock, felt piano, a soft woodblock tick that falls away at the bridge, a lower voice and a higher voice trading verses and meeting in close harmony on the refrain, voices forward and every word clear, no drum kit, a soft glassy bell-tone motif, a long quiet tail.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Intro - strings from near silence, the cello pulse alone]
[Verse 1 - lower voice solo]
I came because the hour came round,
The way it always does;
I wrote that I was here, and found
I could not say I was.
[Verse 2 - higher voice solo]
I meant it well; I always mean;
The meaning cost me none.
But what I meant is never seen,
Just what I've said and done.
[Refrain - both voices in close harmony]
Not what I hoped, not what I planned,
Not what I meant to do:
Let me be measured where I stand
By what came through to you.
[Verse 3 - lower voice solo, the first question]
First ask: with what I could have known,
Was choosing fair and true?
A careful choice can still go wrong,
A careless one get through.
[Verse 4 - higher voice solo, the second question]
Then ask what happened, as it fell,
Not as I'd have it be:
The part that I can see and tell,
And what I cannot see.
[Verse 5 - lower voice solo, the third question]
And last: who now can make it right,
Who has the means to mend?
Not who is blamed, but who has sight,
And hands, and time to spend.
[Refrain - both voices in close harmony]
Not what I hoped, not what I planned,
Not what I meant to do:
Let me be measured where I stand
By what came through to you.
[Bridge - half-spoken over strings, the pulse falls silent]
What could I know? What came of it?
Who now can mend? Then mend.
[Verse 6 - higher voice solo]
I leave a line for one who'll come
To read when I am gone:
Not pretty, but the honest sum
Of done and left undone.
[Verse 7 - lower voice solo, the higher voice joining on the last line]
The clock can bring me to the door;
It cannot make me stay.
I'll count no showing-up as more
Than what I gave away.
[Final Refrain - both voices, fullest, then fading]
Not what we hoped, not what we planned,
Not what we meant to do:
Let us be measured where we stand
By what came through to you.
[Outro - strings thin to the bell-tone, then silence]
