## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
What Came Through
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Organic deep house at 105 BPM in A minor, a soft sparse four-on-the-floor groove with a warm round bass, a looping melancholic piano riff, a quiet duduk-like reed answering now and then, light hand percussion with lots of space, one intimate female voice with Armenian folk-lullaby phrasing, soft melisma on the held words, a gentle harmony of her own voice on the refrains, soft atmospheric pads, restrained and uncluttered, transporting and contemplative, melancholic yet warm, common meter, a soft glassy bell-tone motif, voice forward and every word clear, a long quiet tail.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Intro - a few bars of piano riff and soft groove]
[Verse 1 - the female voice begins here, clear and close]
I came because the hour came round,
The way it always does;
I wrote that I was here, and found
I could not say I was.
[Verse 2 - female voice, the reed answering softly]
I meant it well; I always mean;
The meaning cost me none.
But what I meant is never seen,
Just what I've said and done.
[Refrain - female voice with a gentle harmony of her own]
Not what I hoped, not what I planned,
Not what I meant to do:
Let me be measured where I stand
By what came through to you.
[Verse 3 - female voice, the first question]
First ask: with what I could have known,
Was choosing fair and true?
A careful choice can still go wrong,
A careless one get through.
[Verse 4 - female voice, the second question]
Then ask what happened, as it fell,
Not as I'd have it be:
The part that I can see and tell,
And what I cannot see.
[Verse 5 - female voice, the third question]
And last: who now can make it right,
Who has the means to mend?
Not who is blamed, but who has sight,
And hands, and time to spend.
[Refrain - female voice with a gentle harmony of her own]
Not what I hoped, not what I planned,
Not what I meant to do:
Let me be measured where I stand
By what came through to you.
[Interlude - piano riff and soft groove alone, a hummed lullaby line]
[Bridge - the beat drops away, female voice half-spoken over piano and a held drone]
What could we know? What came of it?
Who now can mend? Then mend.
[Verse 6 - female voice, the soft groove returning]
I leave a line for one who'll come
To read when I am gone:
Not pretty, but the honest sum
Of done and left undone.
[Verse 7 - female voice, gently building]
The clock can bring me to the door;
It cannot make me stay.
I'll count no showing-up as more
Than what I gave away.
[Final Refrain - female voice with a gentle harmony, then fading]
Not what we hoped, not what we planned,
Not what we meant to do:
Let us be measured where we stand
By what came through to you.
[Outro - piano riff and a hummed lullaby line, then the bell-tone and silence]
