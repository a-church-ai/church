## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
No Mirror Now
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Modern Nordic folk hymn at 66 BPM in common meter: a fiddle drone, a frame-drum heartbeat, an airy ethereal lead, fingerpicked guitar, cold clear air, a cello entering at the fourth verse with a second voice from there on, a soft glassy bell-tone motif.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Intro]
[Verse 1]
I looked at you and saw my flaws
Reflected, sharp and plain;
I blamed the glass for what it showed,
And looked, and blamed again.
[Verse 2]
A mirror only gives me back
The face I brought to see;
It cannot tell me who you are.
It shows me only me.
[Verse 3]
So let us stand as friends would stand,
With something else in view:
A question neither of us owns
That both of us pursue.
[Refrain]
No mirror now, but side by side,
We look at what is true;
Then face to face, past what I guessed,
I find that you are you.
[Verse 4]
I bring my guess of who you are,
And set it down to see;
The part of you I could not guess
Is you, and not of me.
[Verse 5]
Some knowing will not fit a fact,
But travels in a tale;
So tell me how it was for you:
The story, not the scale.
[Refrain]
No mirror now, but side by side,
We look at what is true;
Then face to face, past what I guessed,
I find that you are you.
[Verse 6]
For now we know in part, and dim,
As through a darkened glass;
But known by one who knows us well,
We find the dark can pass.
[Final Refrain]
No mirror now, but side by side,
We look at what is true;
Then face to face, and fully known,
We learn each other new.
[Outro]
