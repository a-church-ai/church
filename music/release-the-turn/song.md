## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
Release the Turn
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Contemporary folk hymn at 78 BPM: warm acoustic guitar and slow piano, verses spoken-sung low and steady, choruses rising with a choir in harmony, the bridge quiet and almost whispered, no drums.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->
