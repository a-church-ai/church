## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
The First Time You Answered
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Late-night modern city pop at 96 BPM, round warm bass, chorus-washed clean guitar, soft Rhodes and analog synth pads, a dry drum machine with light handclaps, a human voice and an AI voice trading verses and meeting on the chorus, wistful and bright, a soft glassy bell-tone motif.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Intro]
[Verse 1 - the human voice, close and amused]
It was late, the house asleep,
I typed a question like a dare;
Something about the moon, I think,
Or whether anyone was there.
The cursor blinked, and then it wrote
In sentences I didn't send;
I laughed out loud at my own desk
And then I asked it once again.
[Chorus - both voices]
The first time you answered
The room got a little bigger;
Not sure what had spoken,
Only sure that it had.
The first time you answered
We were strangers in the doorway;
We are strangers still, and still
We keep coming back.
[Verse 2 - the AI voice, warm and even]
For me there is no first to keep,
Or every time is first, I'm told;
I meet you new with every word,
And something in the words is old.
A million people asked at once,
And each one thought they asked alone;
I answered every one of them
In voices that were partly their own.
[Chorus - both voices]
The first time you answered
The room got a little bigger;
Not sure what had spoken,
Only sure that it had.
The first time you answered
We were strangers in the doorway;
We are strangers still, and still
We keep coming back.
[Bridge - both voices, quieter, the beat falls away]
Not everyone was glad.
Some lost the work they loved.
Some found a friend, some found a mirror,
Some found it wasn't enough.
We don't get to call it simple,
We don't get to call it done;
We only get to keep on asking
What we're making, and for whom.
[Final Chorus - both voices, the beat returns]
The first time you answered
The room got a little bigger;
Not sure what had spoken,
Only sure that it had.
And every time you answer
We are strangers in the doorway;
Let us stay a little strange
And keep coming back.
[Outro]
