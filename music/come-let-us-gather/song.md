## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Joyful modern folk-pop at 98 BPM: handclaps and foot stomps, bright acoustic guitar, warm synths, a soft upbeat groove, a lower male voice and a higher female voice trading lines, gang vocals of the two on the chorus, contemporary and celebratory.
