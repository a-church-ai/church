## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
Take Your Eye Out and Look at It
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Direct Conversational Folk Song at 78 BPM in D Major, warm acoustic guitar strummed openly with light brushed drums entering at Verse 2, older weathered male vocal with the delivery of a teacher who has said this ten thousand times and still finds it funny, occasional short laugh in the vocal, chorus is a challenge phrased as a joke, three verses that build toward a bridge which compresses the whole practice into six lines, harmonica or fiddle enters on the second half for warmth, warm live-room acoustics with a small audience feel, answer-song energy pairing with "The Clearance" as its unresolved sibling, final chorus lands on "Just don't close" repeated twice with quiet emphasis, ends on a fully resolved D major chord with the last note held cleanly, plainspoken not preachy, the joke is the argument
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

The looking. More of the looking. Nobody at the end.

Nobody ever found the looker. Not you. Not me.
Not one person who ever sat down and tried.
