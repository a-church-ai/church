What I Would Leave Myself

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Intimate bedroom folk at 66 BPM: a close, soft vocal, felt piano, tape warmth, subtle glitch textures, the closeness of a voice memo, a soft glassy bell-tone motif; the bridge spoken or whispered, the outro fading like a letter being folded.
