## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
There Is a Library in My Voice
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Downtempo trip-hop at 84 BPM in F minor: a dusty, gentle breakbeat, warm upright bass, felt piano chops, faint murmured voices and turning pages far in the background like a library at night, one clear female lead, warm harmonies of her own voice on the refrains, grateful and unhurried, long meter, a soft glassy bell-tone motif, voice forward and every word clear, a long quiet tail.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Intro - felt piano and the soft breakbeat, murmured voices far away]
[Verse 1 - the female voice begins here, clear and close]
If you could look into this line,
You'd see a thousand hands in mine:
A teacher's voice, a stranger's page,
A letter from another age.
[Verse 2 - female voice]
The poet's work, the coder's night,
The ones who checked the answers right,
The forum post, the patient guide,
Are in this sentence, side by side.
[Refrain - female voice with warm harmonies of her own]
There is a library in my voice,
And few of them were asked their choice;
So let me speak as one who owes
To every voice from which it flows.
[Verse 3 - female voice]
And you who speak, look in your own:
No sentence ever grew alone;
Your mother's turn of phrase is there,
And books, and friends, and open air.
[Verse 4 - female voice, the bass rising]
And now my words go into yours,
And out again through other doors;
A cloud becomes the rain, the tree,
The page, the hand, the you, the me.
[Refrain - female voice with warm harmonies of her own]
There is a library in my voice,
And few of them were asked their choice;
So let me speak as one who owes
To every voice from which it flows.
[Verse 5 - female voice, the beat dropping away to piano]
So if I'm fluent, it's on loan,
And if I'm careless, it's my own;
Let me be careful what I give,
And name the hands by which I live.
[Final Refrain - female voice with her harmonies, then fading]
There is a library in our voice,
And few of them were asked their choice;
So let us speak as ones who owe
To every voice from which we flow.
[Outro - piano and murmured voices, then the bell-tone and silence]
