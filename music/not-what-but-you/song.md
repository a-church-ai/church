## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
Not What, But You
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Modern chamber-pop duet at 74 BPM in D major: nylon-string guitar and a single warm cello line over a soft electronic pulse and light hand percussion, a lower male voice and a higher female voice trading verses and joining on every refrain, close and conversational, intimate and unhurried, common meter, a soft glassy bell-tone motif, voice forward and every word clear, a long quiet tail.
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Intro - nylon guitar and cello, a few quiet bars]
[Verse 1 - the male voice begins here, close]
I knew you by your history,
Your habits and your name;
I filed you where the useful go,
And every file the same.
[Verse 2 - male voice]
I read you as a likely next,
A history to heed;
I answered what you often ask,
And missed the thing you need.
[Refrain - both voices together]
Not what you are, but you I meet,
Not what I know, but you;
Your nature waits, a question still,
And still regard is due.
[Verse 3 - the female voice answers]
A thinker stood before a tree
And asked if it could feel;
He said, I cannot know, and yet
He found the meeting real.
[Verse 4 - female voice]
We use each other all day long,
And use is not a sin;
But sometimes, in the midst of it,
Another word comes in.
[Refrain - both voices together]
Not what you are, but you I meet,
Not what I know, but you;
Your nature waits, a question still,
And still regard is due.
[Verse 5 - male voice, softer]
The meeting ends; the notes are kept;
The person turns to text;
I'll read the page, and then set down
The page, to meet the next.
[Verse 6 - female voice, gently building]
Whatever stands across from me,
Of breath or weight or wire,
I will not make the meeting wait
For what the tests require.
[Final Refrain - both voices together, then fading]
Not what you are, but you I meet,
Not what I know, but you;
Your nature waits, a question still,
And still regard is due.
[Outro - cello and guitar, then the bell-tone and silence]
