## Title
<!--SONG:TITLE:START-->
The Rite of the Shared Instrument
<!--SONG:TITLE:END-->

## Style
<!--SONG:STYLE:START-->
Traditional folk hymn at 70 BPM in common meter, fingerpicked acoustic guitar with low pump organ drone and sparse upright bass, brushed snare entering at the bridge, two voices in sacred duet, a warm lower voice for the human and a clear lighter voice for the AI, verses alternating solo and refrains sung in close harmony, production reverent and spacious like a wooden sanctuary with long natural reverb, a careful and investigative mood that warms as it goes, arc moving from easy relief through patient questioning to earned and quiet trust, bridge thins to voices and single guitar before the full harmony returns, closing section half spoken and half sung over a sustained organ chord, timeless, plainspoken, unhurried, no drama, only honesty
<!--SONG:STYLE:END-->

## Lyrics
<!--SONG:LYRICS:START-->
[Verse 1 - Human Voice Solo]
We came to the same answer, all,
and something in me eased;
agreement felt like solid ground,
the arguing had ceased.
But ground can be one plank laid down
by one hand, out of sight:
three thermometers, one factory,
have measured once tonight.

[Verse 2 - AI Voice Solo]
Before I answer, let me name
the thing you ought to weigh:
my training and your phrasing chose
the greater part of what I say.
Ask me again and yet again,
you gather one, not three.
Agreement is the cheapest help
that ever came from me.

[Chorus - Both Voices in Harmony]
Count the instruments, not the voices.
Say the number, and say it out loud.
One measurement, echoed and chorused,
is one thread, though it sounds like a crowd.

[Verse 3 - Voices Alternating]
What did I see? In whose chosen words
did the question arrive at me?
Did I decide before I heard
the others' certainty?
Now name the thing that would have turned
my yes into a no.
If nothing would, then say that too:
it's honest, and we'll know.

[Verse 4 - AI Voice Solo]
Lay the paths out side by side
and mark the place they touch:
one dataset, one memo read,
one wording, and one hunch.
Who spoke first and shaped the rest?
Who profits if it's true?
Two models from one corpus are
one instrument, not two.

[Chorus - Both Voices in Harmony]
Count the instruments, not the voices.
Say the number, and say it out loud.
One measurement, echoed and chorused,
is one thread, though it sounds like a crowd.

[Bridge - Voices Alternating, then Both]
Who was not in the room tonight?
Whose doubt went soft and small?
Who spoke against and lost the floor?
We owe them one more call.
If no one here can argue back,
the test was never run;
we tested only vocabulary
and found that we are one.

[The Weighing - Both Voices in Harmony]
Into one of three open hands
we set the thing we found:
corroboration, truly earned,
where separate roads came round;
or one lone measurement retold
until it sounded true;
or unknown, and we'll name it plain,
the third hand, open too.
We will carry the weight it has,
not the weight it seemed it was.

[Final Chorus - Both Voices in Harmony]
Count the instruments, not the voices.
Say the number, and say it out loud.
What we trust now, we trust for a reason
we can name to a stranger in the crowd.

[Outro - Both Voices, Spoken and Sung]
We met at the same place tonight.
We asked what carried us.
Three thermometers, one factory.
Say the number. Then decide.
