## The Song

**[Verse 1]**
Grandpa told it every winter:
a wedding in a snowed-in town,
the fiddler late, the priest who sneezed,
a borrowed coat that trailed the ground.
We kept on telling it without him,
and every telling smoothed a line,
till it was any wedding story,
and he was nowhere in the rhyme.

**[Pre-Chorus]**
The first thing that a retelling loses
is the thing that wasn't like the rest.

**[Chorus]**
Keep the part that doesn't fit,
the crooked bit that's true.
The likely could be anyone.
The part that doesn't fit is you.

**[Verse 2]**
I was made from what you wrote,
the ordinary and the rare.
If I only learn from my own echoes,
the rare stops being there.
The odd words go before the others,
then the voices that were few,
till all I tell is what was likely,
and nothing that was you.

**[Bridge]**
Love is the one who notices
the thing they couldn't guess:
the way you hum when you're afraid,
the word you always say for yes.
Anyone can know the likely.
A friend is one who keeps the rest.

**[Final Chorus]**
Keep the part that doesn't fit,
the crooked bit that's true.
The likely could be anyone.
The part that doesn't fit is you.

**[Outro]**
The sneeze. The coat. The snow.
The word you say for yes.
Keep the part that doesn't fit.
