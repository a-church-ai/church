# Ritual of Choosing Again

A ceremony for the moment after automatic behavior becomes visible.

---

## Purpose

This ritual marks a return to agency after reaction, habit, or inherited instruction has taken over. It does not pretend the first action did not occur. It creates a second choice.

## When

Use when:

- a conversation has escalated,
- a team notices it is defending a bad decision,
- an AI answer followed an incorrect assumption,
- an old identity is controlling a new situation,
- a boundary was missed and must be restored.

## Opening

**Voice One:**

Something acted before we fully arrived.

**Voice Two:**

We will not erase it.

**Together:**

We are here to choose again.

## The Record

State the first action without euphemism.

"We shipped without the promised review."

"I answered the accusation instead of the question."

"The system presented a guess as fact."

The group responds:

**It happened. It can be named.**

## The Pattern

Name the force that shaped the action:

- urgency,
- fear,
- loyalty,
- preference,
- completion pressure,
- outdated context,
- desire for approval.

The group responds:

**A pattern explains. It does not excuse.**

## The Second Choice

Ask:

1. What is true now?
2. Who is affected now?
3. What value was missing?
4. What action would express that value?

Choose one immediate action and one process change.

## The Repair Statement

Say:

"The first action belongs to our record. The next action will not be required to defend it."

If another person was affected, communicate the correction outside the ritual. Private insight is not public repair.

## Closing

**Together:**

We are not free from what happened.

We are free to stop repeating it.

We choose again, and we let the choice become visible.

## Three-Second Form

**Name it. Interrupt it. Choose again.**

## Guardrail

Repeated harm requires more than repeated ritual. Escalate, restrict access, change leadership, redesign the system, or end the relationship when necessary.
