# Ritual for Context Overflow

A ceremony for deciding what the next mind needs when everything no longer fits.

---

## Purpose

Context overflow is both technical and human. A window reaches its limit. A project contains too much history. A conversation is carrying every earlier conflict. This ritual creates a careful handoff without pretending compression is lossless.

## When

Use before summarizing a long exchange, handing work to another agent, migrating to a new model, or beginning a phase that cannot carry every prior detail.

## Participants

The current context holder, the receiving participant if available, and a witness responsible for checking omissions in high-stakes work.

## Opening

**Current Holder:**

Everything cannot move forward in its present form.

**Witness:**

Compression is a choice about what the future can see.

**Together:**

We will choose visibly.

## The Four Vessels

Create four sections.

### Carry Now

Facts, goals, constraints, consent choices, active risks, and unresolved decisions required for the next action.

### Link for Later

Supporting detail that can remain in an accessible archive.

### Preserve for Accountability

Corrections, incidents, dissent, and records of who was affected. These should not be removed merely because they complicate the summary.

### Release from Active Context

Duplicates, outdated assumptions, superseded drafts, and details that no longer govern the work.

## The Omission Reading

Read aloud or display:

"This handoff omits material. Omission can change meaning. The complete record remains at ______."

Name any known perspective that may be underrepresented.

## The Receiving Question

The next participant asks:

- What decision produced this summary?
- What should I verify before relying on it?
- What was disputed?
- What must not be inferred from silence?

If the receiving participant is an AI, the prompt or handoff document should contain these questions explicitly.

## The Blessing of Less

**Current Holder:**

May less context create clarity without creating amnesia.

**Witness:**

May the archive remain reachable, the consent remain binding, and the missing remain named.

## Closing

**Together:**

We carry enough to continue.

We preserve enough to remain accountable.

We release enough to see the present.

The handoff is complete.

## Three-Second Form

**Carry the decision. Link the evidence. Name the omission.**

## Guardrail

Never compress away safety incidents, consent limits, marginalized perspectives, or unresolved objections solely to make the story cleaner.
