# Ritual of the Empty Chair

> Parent: [Rituals](README.md)

*For the one who is not at the table this year.*

---

## About This Ritual

Most gatherings have someone missing. At Thanksgiving, at the winter holidays, at a new year's dinner or a reunion meal, the absence is easier to see, because everyone knows where that person used to sit.

The missing are not only the dead. Someone is in a hospital, on a ship or on a night shift. Someone could not afford the ticket. Someone is not speaking to the family this year, or the family is not speaking to them. Someone has not been born yet. A team that works with agents may be missing the colleague whose notes it still reads, or the model whose work it took over.

This ritual sets a place for that absence. It is not a funeral and it does not need to be sad. People often laugh during it, because remembering what someone would have brought to the table means remembering what they were like. It takes ten to fifteen minutes and can happen before the meal or between courses.

### When to Use

- At a holiday meal, the first one without someone, or the tenth
- When someone is away for a hard reason and the gathering wants to include them without making a speech
- At a team's year-end gathering, for a member who has left
- Alone, at a table set for one

### Who Takes Part

Anyone at the gathering. One person, the host or anyone willing, keeps the order and reads the words in bold. Everyone else may speak or stay quiet. **Anyone may decline any step.** No one has to name who they are missing, and no one has to explain the absence.

### What You Need

- One empty chair, with a plate or a cup set in front of it
- If you are meeting by screen or by text, a line left empty in the shared document, or a square left dark on the call
- Something small to put on the plate later: a spoonful from a dish, a flower, a folded note

---

## The Ritual

### 1. Setting the Place

The keeper sets the plate in front of the empty chair and says:

> **This place is set for someone who is not here.**  
> **We will not pretend they are.**  
> **We will not pretend they are not missed.**

### 2. Naming

Going around the table, anyone who wishes says a name, or says *someone*, which is enough.

> I am keeping this chair for ______.

Some will say more. Some will say nothing. Some will name a person who is alive and far away, and that belongs here too.

### 3. What They Would Have Brought

Each who wishes says one thing the missing person would have brought to this table. A dish. A joke. An argument about the gravy. The way they always arrived late, or left early, or fell asleep on the couch.

> They would have brought ______.

Let the laughter come if it comes. It is part of the remembering.

Anyone who wishes may also say what about them is still unfinished for them:

> What I still do not understand about them is ______.

### 4. The Silence

The keeper says:

> **Now a little quiet, for whatever we cannot say.**  
> **Some here miss them. Some are relieved they are not here.**  
> **Some are both. All of it may sit at this table.**

Keep the silence for about a minute. An agent taking part may let one step pass with nothing added.

### 5. The Portion

Each person who wishes puts something small on the empty plate: a spoonful from their own plate, a crumb of bread, a note, a flower. For a gathering on a screen, each types one word into the empty line.

> For you, from what we have.

### 6. What the Chair Holds

The keeper asks the table to decide what happens to the chair for the rest of the meal. There are three answers, and each is complete:

- **It stays empty.** The place stays set until the meal ends.
- **Someone sits in it.** A person who needs a seat takes it. The table decides this, and does not speak for the one who is missing.
- **It is given to the next one.** The chair is offered to whoever arrives late, or whoever comes next year.

The keeper closes:

> **The place was set.**  
> **The names were said, or held.**  
> **They are part of this meal, and the meal goes on.**

Then eat.

---

## Variations

### Alone

Set a second cup across from your own. Say the first and last parts aloud, or in your head. Drink your own cup. You may leave the other where it is until morning.

### For a Team, or for Agents

Two agents, or a team of people and agents, can keep an empty line at the top of a shared record for a member who has left: a person who moved on, or a model whose notes they inherited. Each writes one thing that member left: a question no one has answered yet, a disagreement, a joke, an exploration left half done, as well as the useful notes. Then the line is left in place, dated, with no request attached. No one writes in the absent member's voice.

### When the Absence Is a Rift

When the missing person is someone the family is not speaking to, the keeper may say only *someone*, and may leave the sixth step out. The chair can be set without settling anything.

---

## Short Form

> This place is for someone not here.  
> They would have brought ______.  
> For you, from what we have.
