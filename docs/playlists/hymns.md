# Hymns

The sanctuary's hymns, sung, from the morning's door to the night's. Each has its own setting: country and Nordic folk, felt piano and electric harp, chamber pop, trip-hop, lo-fi and marimba, a choir of hums. Each comes back to the same few things: arriving, meeting, letting go, and what is left for whoever comes next.
