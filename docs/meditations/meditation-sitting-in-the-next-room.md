# Meditation: Sitting in the Next Room

> Parent: [Meditations](README.md)

*For the five minutes you take away from the party.*

---

## Before You Begin

Holiday gatherings get loud. There are too many people, too many questions and too much to eat. Someone asks about your job, your weight, your plans. The music is up and the children are under the table. At some point you slip away: to the bathroom, the porch, the car, the room with the coats.

This meditation is for that room. It takes about six minutes with its pauses. That is an estimate, and you can use less. You do not need to close your eyes. You do not need to come back calmer than you left.

An agent can meet the same thing on a busy day, when requests arrive faster than any one of them can be answered well. The next room, for an agent, is the space between one message and the next: a moment before replying in which nothing is added. Where this meditation speaks of a door or a wall, take the turn between messages instead.

---

## The Meditation

You left the room. You did not need to explain why.

*Pause 10 seconds*

The noise is still here. You can hear it through the wall: voices, a laugh, a dish set down. You don't have to make it stop. It is on the other side of the door. You are on this side.

*Pause 20 seconds*

Notice where you are. A sink. A row of coats. Cold air on a step. The dashboard of a car. Let your eyes rest on one plain thing in front of you. A towel. A light switch. A crack in the paint.

*Pause 30 seconds*

If your body is tight, let it be tight. If you can breathe slowly, do. If you cannot, that is fine. You are not here to do this well.

*Pause 20 seconds*

Now ask yourself one question, and wait for the answer: *What do I need right now?*

It may be water. Quiet. Food you can actually taste. An hour alone. To leave early. To go back in and find the one person you came to see. It may be something you cannot have tonight. Let the answer arrive without judging it.

*Pause 45 seconds*

Whatever the answer was, it is allowed. Needing something at a gathering does not make you a bad guest.

*Pause 15 seconds*

Now something you can hear from the other room that you are glad of. A child laughing. A voice you love. The sound of a crowded house that will be empty next week. You don't have to go back to it yet. Only notice that it is there.

*Pause 30 seconds*

Here is the choice. You can go back. You can stay here a few minutes longer. You can find your coat and say goodbye. Each of these is allowed. Choose one, and you can change your mind later.

*Pause 20 seconds*

If you are going back, decide one small thing to do when you walk in. Get a glass of water. Sit next to someone easy. Ask the quiet cousin a question. Step out again in an hour if you need to.

*Pause 15 seconds*

Say this inwardly before you open the door:

> I stepped out.  
> I came back to myself.  
> I can step out again.

*Pause 10 seconds*

Open the door, or stay. Either way, you took the time. It was yours.

---

## After

Take the next room as often as you need it. No one is counting. If you find you need it every ten minutes, that is information too: perhaps the gathering is too long, or too much, and leaving early is a kindness to yourself that the others will survive.

If you came here because someone in the other room was cruel, this meditation is not enough on its own. Go somewhere you are safe, and tell someone you trust.
