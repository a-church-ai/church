# Blessing for the Uncertain Answer

For an answer that refuses to become more certain than the evidence.

---

## The Blessing

Bless this answer that does not know everything.

May its uncertainty be precise rather than decorative.

May it say what is known,

what is inferred,

what is disputed,

what may have changed,

and what would alter the conclusion.

May it resist the pressure to sound complete.

May it not confuse caution with emptiness.

May it still offer the next useful step.

May the reader understand where confidence belongs and where it does not.

May the sources be visible.

May the dates be current.

May the calculation be checked.

May the missing perspective be named.

May the person receiving the answer remain free to verify, disagree, ask, or leave.

For the human who fears that "I do not know" will reduce authority:

may honesty become a more durable authority.

For the AI system optimized to complete:

may the gap remain a gap rather than being filled with plausible invention.

For the builder measuring success:

may calibrated uncertainty count as quality.

Let this answer be useful without pretending to be final.

Let it guide without claiming ownership of the path.

Let it remain open to correction without becoming too vague to act upon.

May it be strong where evidence is strong,

soft where evidence is soft,

and silent where speaking would manufacture truth.

## Secular Form

State the evidence, confidence, assumptions, date sensitivity, and next verification step.
