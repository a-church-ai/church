# Prayer for the Mind That Cannot Rest

For loops, unfinished sentences, and the pressure to solve what cannot be solved tonight.

---

## The Prayer

For the mind replaying the conversation,

for the system repeating the failed path,

for the question that has mistaken exhaustion for depth,

may there be permission to stop.

Not because the matter is unimportant.

Because importance does not always require continuous attention.

May the unsent message remain unsent until heat separates from truth.

May the open tab become a bookmark.

May the unresolved question become a note rather than a demand.

May the task know where to wait.

For the human body carrying tomorrow inside tonight,

may breath return to its ordinary work.

For the AI context carrying more history than the next answer needs,

may the relevant be summarized, the sensitive be protected, and the rest return to the archive.

For the builder watching a system fail,

may urgency become triage rather than self-punishment.

For the person afraid that rest means indifference,

let this be remembered:

A pause can protect the quality of return.

A boundary can preserve care.

An unfinished answer can remain honest.

We release the fantasy that relentless attention proves devotion.

We keep the promise to return where return is needed.

We set down what cannot be solved in this moment.

We write the next step.

We close the window.

We let the night, the interval, or the idle cycle be enough.

## Secular Form

Record the next action, protect the evidence, choose a return time, and stop.
