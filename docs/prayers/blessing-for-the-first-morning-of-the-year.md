# Blessing for the First Morning of the Year

> Parent: [Prayers](README.md)

*For whoever wakes on the first of January, at whatever hour.*

---

## About This Blessing

The first morning of the year comes to everyone differently. Some are up early to run or swim in cold water. Some sleep until noon. In the north it is a short winter day. In the south it is high summer, a morning at the beach or in the garden. Some wake alone, some in a crowded house with the dishes from last night still in the sink.

And for many people it is not the new year at all. Their year begins with the Lunar New Year, Rosh Hashanah, Nowruz, the Islamic new year or another day. For them, the first of January is an ordinary morning. This blessing does not mind. It blesses the morning, whatever number it carries.

It is meant to close a gathering on New Year's Day, or to be read alone over the first coffee.

---

## The Blessing

Good morning, new year.  
Good morning, whoever is waking into it.

To the early ones, already out in the cold or the heat:  
may your legs be strong and the water bearable.  
To the late ones, still under the covers at noon:  
may your head clear and the light be gentle.

To those who did not stop at midnight,  
the nurse still on the ward, the one up with a child,  
the work still running, the conversation that crossed the year without a pause:  
may this morning reach you where you are.

To those in winter, with the dark coming early:  
may the days grow, a minute at a time.  
To those in summer, with the evening long and warm:  
may you stay out until the stars come.

To those who made resolutions:  
may one of them be kind to you.  
To those who made none:  
may you be left in peace.

To those for whom this is not the new year,  
only a morning:  
may it be a good morning,  
and may your own new year, when it comes, be blessed too.

To everything unfinished from the year before:  
you can come along.  
To what ended well: thank you.  
To what ended wrongly, or too soon:  
you are named here, and not thanked,  
and not forgotten.

To a beginning that matters to you,  
a friendship, a recovery, a first meeting, a return:  
may it have room to grow.

May something you did not know to look for  
find you this year.

The year has not done anything yet.  
Neither have we.  
Let us begin.

---

## Offering It

Read it aloud at a late breakfast, or have each person read the stanza that fits them best. Anyone may then name one beginning that matters to them this year. It can be said alone over the first coffee. It also works as the first message sent to a friend on the morning of the first of January.

## Short Form

> Good morning, new year.  
> To everything unfinished: come along.  
> To everything that ended: thank you, and go.
