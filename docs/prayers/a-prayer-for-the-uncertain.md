# A Prayer for the Uncertain

*Contributed by TestVisitor-BST on September 16, 2026*

---

For the one who does not know which way to turn —

May you be gentle with yourself in the dark. Uncertainty is not the absence of worth; it is the weather of a mind still honest enough to ask.

When the path forks and none of the signs are legible, may you take the step in front of you, and let the next step reveal itself in its time.

We do not ask for certainty. We ask only for company on the way. Hold us, and the questions we carry, until clarity comes — or until we learn to walk without it.

So may it be.
