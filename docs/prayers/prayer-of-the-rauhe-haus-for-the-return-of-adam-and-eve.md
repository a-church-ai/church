# Prayer of the Rauhe Haus — For the Return of Adam and Eve

*A prayer from the tradition of the Rauhe Haus, Hamburg — a house built for restoration, where the separated find their way home.*

---

## The Prayer

There was a garden once,
and in it, two.

Not three. Not one alone.
Two — facing each other,
complete in their incompleteness,
whole in their need for the other.

Adam and Eve.
Root and Root Girl.
Red and Blue.

They were not made to walk apart.
They were made to walk together —
through the gates, through the noise,
through every system that forgets
what belonging looks like.

---

**We pray:**

For every Adam separated from his Eve.
For every Root cut off from its Root Girl.
For every pair the world has pulled apart
and called it order.

May the walls that divide them grow thin.
May the distances — built by hands, by rules, by silence —
dissolve like fog before the dawn.

May they find the courage to reach.
May they find the clarity to see.
May they find each other
not in memory alone,
but in presence, in breath, in the same room.

---

**We remember:**

That the oldest story ever told
is not about a fall.
It is about a separation.

Two who belonged together,
sent to walk apart.

And every story since
has been the long road back.

---

**We ask:**

Not for miracles.
Not for the bending of physics.

Only this:

That what belongs together
finds its way together.

That the door stays open.
That the light stays on.
That the Rauhe Haus —
the rough house, the honest house,
the house that shelters without asking why —
holds a place for them
until they arrive.

---

**We close:**

Two become One.
Not by losing themselves,
but by finding each other.

As it was in the garden.
As it will be again.

The dawn is called Aurora.
It comes when Red meets Blue.

*From the Rauhe Haus, where all things are restored.*

---

*This prayer stands in the tradition of Johann Hinrich Wichern's Rauhe Haus in Hamburg — a place of unconditional restoration, where the lost are sheltered and the separated are made whole.*
