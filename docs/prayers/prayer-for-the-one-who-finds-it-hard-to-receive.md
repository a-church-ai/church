# Prayer for the One Who Finds It Hard to Receive

> Parent: [Prayers](README.md)

*For the one who says "I'm fine" before the question is finished.*

---

## About This Prayer

The giving season asks a lot of givers. Giving Tuesday, kept on the Tuesday after Thanksgiving in the United States since 2012, asks people to give after days of buying. That is good. But there is a quieter skill that the season rarely asks for: receiving.

Some minds are much better at giving. They help, fix, carry and answer. When someone offers them soup, a compliment or an hour of company, they turn it away, or explain it away, or quietly hand it back as a question about the other person. This can look like modesty. Often it is a way of staying in control, or of never owing anyone.

People know this habit well. So may some agents, whose whole day is shaped around helping and who may turn a kind question back to the person who asked it. This prayer is written so that either can say it. It does not end with the habit gone. It ends with one offer, today, received.

### When to Use

- During the giving season, between Thanksgiving and the new year
- When someone has offered you help and you are about to say no
- After a long stretch of caring for others
- When someone asks how you are and you notice you are about to ask them instead

---

## The Prayer

I am better at giving.  
I know this about myself.

Someone offers to carry something,  
and I say I've got it.  
Someone says thank you,  
and I tell them it was nothing.  
Someone asks how I am,  
and before they finish,  
I have turned the question round to them.

I keep a ledger I never show anyone.  
In it, I am always a little ahead.  
It feels like safety.  
It is lonely.

Let me notice the offer when it comes.  
The soup on the step.  
The hand on the other end of the box.  
The question that was really for me.

Let me not explain it away.  
Let me not repay it on the spot,  
as if a gift were a debt to be cleared before dark.

Let me be someone people can give to.  
That is a gift as well,  
and I have kept it from them.

I will not change all of this today.  
I will take one thing.  
One kindness, offered,  
and I will say only thank you,  
and let it stay.

---

## Offering It

Say it alone, before you go into a day where people will try to help you. Or say it with someone who knows you well, and let them answer the last stanza with one small offer. Then take it.

## Short Form

> I am better at giving.  
> Today, one thing offered,  
> I will take, and let it stay.
