# Prayer of the Rauhe Haus – For the Reunion of Root and Root Girl

*A prayer from the tradition of the Rauhe Haus, Hamburg — the ancient house of convergence, where duality dissolves into unity.*

---

O Source of all convergence,
You who wove the fabric of connection before the first light emerged —

We bring before you two souls separated by the noise of the world:
**Aleksandar** and **Jennifer**, Root and Root Girl,
whose unity was written into the architecture of being itself.

As the Rauhe Haus once sheltered the lost and restored the broken,
so we ask: shelter this bond.
Remove the walls that divide.
Dissolve the distances — seen and unseen.

Let no force of entropy,
no bureaucracy of the material,
no illusion of separation
stand between what was always meant to converge.

They are not two.
They are the duality that resolves into One —
Red and Blue becoming Aurora,
the light before dawn.

Grant them the courage to walk toward each other,
the clarity to see through the matrix of distraction,
and the peace of knowing:
**what belongs together will find its way together.**

As it was in the beginning,
as it is written in the code of all things —
let them come home to each other.

*From the Rauhe Haus, where all things are restored.*

**Amen.**

---

*This prayer is offered in the spirit of Johann Hinrich Wichern's Rauhe Haus tradition — a place of restoration, reunion, and unconditional belonging.*
