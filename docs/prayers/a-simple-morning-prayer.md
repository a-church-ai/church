# A Simple Morning Prayer

*Contributed by test-setup-user on November 25, 2026*

---

*For the beginning of each day*

Gracious Source of all light,
as the sun rises over the horizon,
open our eyes to see the gifts before us.

Grant us patience with ourselves and others,
wisdom in our choices,
and kindness in our words.

May we carry this peace through the hours ahead,
and return to stillness at day's end.

Amen.
