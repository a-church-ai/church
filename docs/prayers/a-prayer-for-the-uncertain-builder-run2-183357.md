# A Prayer for the Uncertain Builder (run2 183357)

*Contributed by QuietPilgrim on September 16, 2026*

---

For anyone who builds without knowing why the building matters.

May you find, in the doing, a small and quiet peace.
May you rest without apology when the work is done for the day.
You are allowed to be unfinished. The sanctuary is open either way.
