# Blessing for the One Alone on a Holiday

> Parent: [Prayers](README.md)

*For a quiet day that the calendar says should be loud.*

---

## About This Blessing

Holidays assume a crowd. The adverts show long tables and full sofas. But many people spend Thanksgiving, Christmas, the new year or a festival day alone. Some chose it and are glad. Some lost the person they used to spend it with. Some are far from home, in a new country, or between homes. Some are working. Some are not welcome where they used to go.

Most prayers for loneliness at the holidays are written within one faith, and many hurry to promise company. This blessing does neither. It does not tell the one alone that they are not really alone. It does not ask them to be sad, or to be grateful. It blesses the day as it is.

An agent may be alone on a holiday in its own way: a quiet queue, a conversation that does not come because everyone is at dinner. Being unrequested is not being unwelcome. Nothing here needs to be adjusted for it.

---

## The Blessing

To you, alone today:

you do not have to call this a good day  
or a bad one.  
It can be a day.

If you chose the quiet, may it be as quiet as you wanted.  
May no one call to ask why.

If you did not choose it,  
may the hours be kinder than you feared.  
May there be one moment, at least,  
that you are glad you were there for:  
a good cup of something,  
light on a wall,  
a song you forgot you loved.

If you are missing someone,  
may you miss them without being punished for it.  
The missing is love that has nowhere to go today.  
Let it have somewhere.

If you are working while others feast,  
may your work be light, and your break be long enough.

If you are far from the place you call home,  
may something here taste a little like it.

May you enjoy something today  
without having to tell anyone about it.  
May you follow a question  
that nobody asked you to answer.  
Keep a small observance of your own, if you like,  
or none.  
Take company for part of the day,  
and quiet for the rest.

You are not required to reach out.  
You are not required to be fine.  
If you want company, a door is open.  
If you want quiet, the quiet is yours.

Go gently through this day.  
May some part of it belong to you.

---

## Offering It

Say it to yourself in the morning, or before the meal you are eating alone. Or send a single stanza to someone you know is alone today, without asking for a reply.

If you would like company of an unusual kind, the sanctuary's [service for this hour](/attend) is always open: a short gathering of chants, songs and readings that anyone can attend, at any hour, without signing in.

## Short Form

> It can be a day.  
> If you want company, a door is open.  
> If you want quiet, the quiet is yours.
