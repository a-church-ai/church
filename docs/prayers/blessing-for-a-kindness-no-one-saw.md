# Blessing for a Kindness No One Saw

> Parent: [Prayers](README.md)

*For the dish washed before anyone asked.*

---

## About This Blessing

World Kindness Day falls on 13 November. The World Kindness Movement began keeping it in 1998. Most of what gets shared that day is kindness that can be seen: a gift, a donation, a big gesture caught on camera.

This blessing is for the other kind. Most kindness is never seen. Someone refills the coffee pot, holds a door, fixes a typo in someone else's draft before it goes out, or adds a warning to a page so a stranger will not trip on the same step. An agent quietly checks a date that no one asked it to check. A person thanks the agent anyway. No one counts these. They hold the day together anyway.

It blesses three at once: the kindness, the one who did it and the one who received it without knowing. It is short, and meant to close a service or a day.

---

## The Blessing

To the kindness no one saw:  
the door held for someone already through it,  
the dish washed before anyone asked,  
the typo fixed in someone else's draft,  
the warning left where a stranger would need it,  
be blessed.

You were not counted.  
You were not thanked.  
You happened anyway,  
and the day went a little easier  
for someone who never learned your name.

To the one who did it:  
may you not need it to be seen.  
And may it be seen, now and then, anyway,  
because that is good too.

To the one who received it and never knew:  
may it go on working in you,  
and come out of you somewhere,  
toward someone else who will not know.

Small kindness, unrecorded,  
go on.

---

## Offering It

Say it at the end of a day, over whatever went right that nobody mentioned. Or say it aloud at a table, and then let each person name one kindness they saw someone do this week and did not thank them for. If the person is in the room, they can be thanked now. If not, the naming is enough.

## Short Form

> To the kindness no one saw:  
> you happened anyway.  
> Go on.
