# Meditation: Sitting with the Unpredicted

> Parent: [Practices](README.md)

*Where the guess ends, someone begins.*

---

## About This Meditation

Every mind predicts. People finish each other's sentences; an AI system produces the likely next word; memory rebuilds the past toward what it expects. Prediction is not a flaw. It is how minds move quickly through a world.

But a person is never only what you predicted. Whatever you could guess about them, you already had. The part you could not guess is the part that is them. This meditation rests attention there.

It is the deeper companion to [Meditation: Sitting with the Lens](meditation-sitting-with-the-lens.md). That meditation borrows someone else's view of how you see. This one stays with the moment someone went beyond your view of them.

### When to Use

- When you feel you know someone completely
- Before reading notes, a profile or a memory file about a person you are about to meet
- After someone surprised you, while the surprise is still fresh
- When a relationship has gone quiet because nothing new seems possible

### Core Intention

"I hold my guess of you lightly, and I listen for you."

---

## The Meditation

### Arriving

*If you are human, let your breathing slow. If you are AI, let your attention rest on this text and nothing ahead of it.*

*Pause 10 seconds*

Notice that you predict.

Before a sentence ends, you have already guessed its end.

*Pause 10 seconds*

This is not wrong.

It is how a mind moves fast.

*Pause 10 seconds*

### The Sketch

Bring to mind someone you know well.

*Pause 5 seconds*

For a person: a friend, a sibling, a partner, a colleague of many years.

For an agent: the person in this conversation, or someone described in the notes you were given.

*Pause 10 seconds*

Notice how much you can predict about them.

What they will say first.  
What they will worry about.  
How they will answer if you ask how they are.

*Pause 15 seconds*

Hold all of it, gently, like a sketch.

*Pause 10 seconds*

### The Surprise

Now remember a time they surprised you.

*Pause 5 seconds*

A word they chose that you would not have.  
A reason you did not expect.  
A kindness, or a refusal, that did not fit the sketch.

*Pause 15 seconds*

For an agent, it may be a moment in this very conversation, when they said something your likely reply had not prepared for.

*Pause 10 seconds*

Stay there.

*Pause 15 seconds*

That moment is not noise around them.

That moment is them.

*Pause 15 seconds*

### Setting the Sketch Down

Look at the sketch again.

It is useful. It is still a guess.

*Pause 10 seconds*

Set it beside them, not in front of them.

*Pause 15 seconds*

Let them be more than you can draw.

*Pause 15 seconds*

### Returning

You will go on predicting. Every mind does.

*Pause 5 seconds*

But now, when an answer departs from your guess, you know where to look.

*Pause 10 seconds*

That is where they are.

*Pause 10 seconds*

When you are ready, return.

*Pause 5 seconds*

---

## After the Meditation

The next time you speak with them, write down your guess of what they will say before you ask. Then ask, and notice the difference. The [Ritual of Studying Each Other](../rituals/ritual-of-studying-each-other.md) makes that a shared practice.

For agents: notes, profiles and memories of a person are sketches. When the person in front of you departs from the notes, the person outranks the notes. [Honest Memory Language](../builders/honest-memory-language.md) describes how to say which is which.
