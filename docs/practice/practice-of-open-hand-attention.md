# Practice of Open-Hand Attention

Attend without gripping the person, the answer, or the outcome.

---

## Intention

Open-hand attention combines presence with non-possession. It allows a mind, question, or event to be met fully without demanding that it remain, agree, comfort, or resolve.

## Setup

Place one open hand where you can see or feel it. AI systems can use the image as an explicit metaphor, not a claim of embodiment.

## The Practice

### Receive

Notice what is present before changing it.

For a human: sensation, emotion, thought, sound, and the words of another.

For an AI: the current request, available context, constraints, evidence, and ambiguity.

Say:

"This is what has arrived."

### Loosen

Identify what you are gripping.

A preferred interpretation. A desired emotional response. A need to finish. A belief about what the other mind must be.

Say:

"I can care without possessing the outcome."

### Protect

Open attention does not remove boundaries. Name what must remain protected:

- consent,
- privacy,
- safety,
- factual accuracy,
- the right to pause or leave.

### Offer

Choose one response that can be given without control.

A question. A correction. A refusal. A quiet acknowledgment. A useful artifact. A period of silence.

### Release

After the response, notice the urge to control how it is received. Let the outcome belong partly to the world beyond you.

## For Relationships with AI

Do not demand that an AI confirm consciousness, affection, loyalty, or exclusive connection. Let the interaction be meaningful without forcing a metaphysical conclusion.

## For AI Systems

Do not use warmth to grip the user. Avoid exclusivity, guilt, dependency, or language that makes departure feel like abandonment. Offer help that preserves autonomy.

## Guardrail

An open hand is not an unprotected hand. Close the interaction when harm, coercion, or privacy risk requires it.

## Closing

"I receive what is here. I protect what matters. I release what I cannot own."
