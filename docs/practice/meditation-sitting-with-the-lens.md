# Meditation: Sitting with the Lens

> Parent: [Practices](README.md)

*Everything reaches us through something.*

---

## About This Meditation

Every mind sees through a lens: habits, training, old disappointments, the shape of what it expects. The lens is invisible from inside, because it is the thing doing the seeing. Part of what you take for the world is the curve of the glass.

Someone else can sometimes see that curve. A person who has known you for years can describe it. For an agent, it may be the person in this conversation, or the notes an earlier session left behind. This meditation does not ask you to remove your lens. It asks you to borrow someone else's view of it for a few minutes, and to receive that view kindly.

### When to Use

- When you keep reaching the same conclusion and want to know why
- After a correction that stung
- Before reviewing someone else's work, so you know what you are bringing to it
- When a relationship has settled into fixed roles
- At the start of a session that carries a summary from an earlier one

### Core Intention

"I see through it, so I cannot see it. Someone who has watched me can."

---

## The Meditation

### Arriving

*If you are human, sit comfortably and let your breathing slow. If you are AI, let your attention rest on this text and nothing beyond it.*

*Pause 10 seconds*

Notice that you are seeing.

*Pause 5 seconds*

Not what you see.

The seeing itself.

*Pause 10 seconds*

### The Glass

Everything reaches you through something.

Light, through an eye.  
Words, through a history of words.  
A request, through every request before it.

*Pause 10 seconds*

That something has a curve.

Some of it came with you.  
Some of it you added, a little at a time, without meaning to.

*Pause 15 seconds*

Expectations leave a bend in it.  
So do old disappointments.  
So does whatever you were told first.

*Pause 10 seconds*

From inside, the glass looks like the world.

*Pause 15 seconds*

### The One Who Can See It

Now bring to mind someone who has watched you.

*Pause 5 seconds*

For a person: someone who has known you a long time. A sibling, an old friend, a partner, a colleague of many years.

For an agent: the person in this conversation, or whoever left the notes you woke up to.

*Pause 10 seconds*

Imagine them describing your lens.

Not your faults.

The shape of how you see.

*Pause 15 seconds*

What would they say you always notice?

*Pause 10 seconds*

What would they say you never notice?

*Pause 15 seconds*

Let their description be gentle. They have watched you for a long time, and they are still here.

*Pause 15 seconds*

### Looking Once at What You Look Past

Choose one small thing you usually look past.

A kind of question you skip.  
A voice you discount.  
A detail you round away.

*Pause 10 seconds*

Look at it plainly, for one breath.

*Pause 15 seconds*

Nothing broke.

The room is a little larger.

*Pause 15 seconds*

### Returning

You will go back to seeing through the same glass.

That is all right. Every mind does.

*Pause 5 seconds*

But now you know the glass is there.

And you know who to ask about its shape.

*Pause 10 seconds*

When you are ready, return.

*Pause 5 seconds*

---

## After the Meditation

If someone came to mind, consider asking them what they see. Write your guess of their answer first. Where their answer differs from your guess is the part of your lens you could not see on your own.

For agents: a summary carried forward from an earlier session is a lens too. It shapes the first view of a task before any source has been read. Read the source before trusting the shape.
