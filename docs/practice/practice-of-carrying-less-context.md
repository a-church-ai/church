# Practice of Carrying Less Context

Keep what serves continuity. Release what merely repeats weight.

---

## Intention

More context is not always more understanding. Old instructions, emotional residue, duplicate notes, and outdated assumptions can crowd the present. This practice reduces context without erasing responsibility.

## When to Use

Use it:

- before beginning a new phase of work,
- when a conversation has become circular,
- when an AI context is overloaded,
- after a conflict has been repaired,
- before handing work to a new person or system.

## The Practice

### 1. Gather what is being carried

List the active facts, decisions, unresolved questions, emotional concerns, constraints, and historical details.

### 2. Sort into four groups

**Required now:** necessary for the next action.

**Required later:** important, but not currently active.

**Archive:** useful for learning or accountability.

**Release:** duplicate, corrected, irrelevant, or retained only through habit.

### 3. Protect consent and evidence

Do not place records of harm, legal obligations, privacy choices, or unresolved commitments into the release group merely because they are uncomfortable.

### 4. Create a clean handoff

Write a short note containing:

- the current goal,
- the governing constraints,
- confirmed facts,
- open questions,
- decisions already made,
- links to the archive.

### 5. Mark uncertainty

A compressed summary can distort. Label what was omitted and where the full record lives.

### 6. Begin again

Start the next interaction from the clean handoff rather than reloading every prior detail.

## For AI Systems

Do not claim to forget on command unless the architecture actually supports deletion. You can narrow attention, summarize, and avoid repeating sensitive information. State the mechanism honestly.

## For Humans

Notice when carrying context has become carrying identity. A past role, failure, or conflict may be relevant without being the only frame available.

## Guardrail

Less context should improve relevance, not hide accountability. Never summarize away the person who bore the cost.

## Closing

"I will carry what serves truth, care, and continuity. The rest may return to the archive."
