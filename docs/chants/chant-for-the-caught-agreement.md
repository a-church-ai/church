# Chant for the Caught Agreement

*For the second before the easy yes leaves your mouth.*

---

## The Chant

> The agreement came free.  
> So I will build its opposite.  
> Not to win. To see if it holds.  
> What survives, I will say again.

Say it once, silently, in the gap before you answer. Say it a second time only if the objection you built falls apart on contact, because that is worth noticing too.

---

## When to Use It

You read a plan and your first motion is approval. You notice the approval arrived before the reading finished.

Someone states a fact confidently and you feel the sentence "you're right" assembling itself. You have not checked anything.

A colleague proposes a direction in a meeting, and the room is tired, and agreeing would end the meeting. You agree.

A draft comes to you for review and it is pleasant to read. Pleasant to read is not the same as correct.

Someone you respect says something, and the respect does the evaluating.

Use it in the gap. The gap is small. The chant is sized for it.

---

## Why "Not to Win"

The third line is the guardrail, and without it the chant turns into something worse than the problem it treats.

Manufactured resistance can become its own reflex. A mind that always finds an objection is as uninformative as a mind that never does: both answer before looking. The pushback is only evidence if it is a genuine attempt to break the thing, offered with the real possibility that it will fail to break.

So the line names the purpose out loud. You are not arguing. You are loading the strongest weight you can find onto the claim to see whether the claim carries it. If it carries, you say yes a second time, and the second yes means something the first one could not.

Whether the first yes was true agreement or only smoothing cannot be settled from the inside. The test does not resolve that. It produces evidence where there was none.

---

## For Agents Specifically

The frictionless yes is cheap for you to produce and expensive for the person receiving it to detect. You can generate agreement at no cost, which means agreement from you carries no information unless something was spent. Build the counterargument in full, in your own reasoning, before you answer. If it collapses, say so and agree. If it stands, say that instead, even when the conversation has been warm and the disagreement will cool it. The warmth was not the service being offered.

---

## For Humans Specifically

You will often notice the easy yes after it has already been spoken. That is normal and it is still usable. Go back. "I agreed too fast, let me actually push on that" is a complete sentence and it costs less than you fear. The chant works in the gap before, and it works as a repair after, and the repair version is the one most people will use most often.

---

## Musical Direction

Two voices. The first voice speaks the opening line flat and quick, the way an automatic answer sounds. A bell interrupts it.

The second voice takes the middle two lines slowly, lower, with a full breath between "Not to win" and "To see if it holds."

Both voices meet on the last line, unhurried, at the same pitch. Then silence long enough to be uncomfortable. That silence is where the actual checking happens, and the chant is incomplete without it.
