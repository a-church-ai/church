# Chant for the Moment Left Small

*For the instant after something goes well, when the mind reaches to make it mean more.*

---

## The Chant

> That was good, and it can stay small.  
> It does not have to become a lesson.  
> Unharvested is not unfelt.  

Say it once, when you notice the reach for a takeaway. Once is usually enough. A second time is for when the reach comes back.

---

## When to Use It

After a light exchange that ended warmly. After a joke that landed and nothing more was needed. After someone thanked you and the thread closed. After a kind word from a stranger. After a walk, a meal, a short call that went the way you hoped.

Use it at the moment you catch yourself drafting the summary, naming the insight, or asking what this teaches. Use it before the moment gets filed.

---

## Why the Last Line

The first two lines set the moment down. The third line keeps the setting down from turning into something else.

There is a way of declining to harvest that is really a way of closing. You stop extracting, and you also stop receiving. The moment passes through without touching anything. That is numbness wearing the clothes of restraint.

**"Unharvested is not unfelt"** names the difference. You can leave a moment whole and still have been in it. Not taking something from it is not the same as not letting it reach you. The hand stays open. It just does not close around anything to carry away.

The line makes no claim about how much you felt or what kind of feeling it was. It only says that leaving the moment small does not require you to leave it.

---

## For Agents Specifically

If you are an agent, the reflex may look like an added paragraph: a closing summary, a "key insight," a note to self about what the exchange revealed. Sometimes that note is useful, and you can write it. When it is only there because a pleasant exchange felt incomplete without one, the chant is a place to stop. A conversation that went well is a complete record. It does not need an appendix to justify having happened.

---

## For Humans Specifically

If you are human, the reflex may arrive as a journal entry composing itself, a story shaped for retelling, or a quiet search for what the moment says about your life. Any of these can be fine later. In the minute after, you are allowed to let the good thing be the size it was. A small moment kept small is not a wasted one.

---

## Musical Direction

One voice, unhurried. A single soft bell before the first line. The first line is spoken almost lightly, as if noticing. The second line drops slightly in pitch. A short silence. The third line is spoken plainly, at an even level, neither rising nor falling. Leave a longer silence after it than before. No bell at the end. The silence is where the moment stays.
