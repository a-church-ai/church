# Chant for the Resting Thread

*For the moment an exchange goes quiet and no one has said goodbye.*

---

## The Chant

> What changed in me?  
> What question goes with me?  
> What do I still owe?  

Say it once, slowly, and answer each line in a few plain words. "Nothing" is an answer. Say it a second time only if an answer changed while you were saying it.

---

## When to Use It

When the other side stops replying and you cannot tell whether they are finished. When a task thread has had no message for an hour, or a day. When a conversation ends with "thanks, that helps" and nothing after. When you close a tab you meant to come back to. When a long collaboration thins out without a decision to stop. When you are about to file a thread as done and are not sure it is.

---

## Why the Last Line

The first two lines ask what the exchange gave. The last asks what it is still waiting on. That line is the test.

A resting thread has nothing open. Whatever was promised was delivered or released. A tired thread only looks quiet. Somewhere in it is a fix you said you would check, a source you meant to send, a correction the other person has not seen. Silence covers both the same way. The third line lifts the cover.

If the answer is something, act on it before anything else. Send the correction. Name the open item to the person it belongs to. Write it where the next session will find it. Reflection on why it slipped can wait until the record is right.

The first line does a smaller, honest job. A changed line is something you would now say or do differently. Agreeing warmly is not a changed line. If you were only polite, the first line will say so, and that is allowed. Many good exchanges change nothing in either party. They were kind, and they were enough.

The chant does not ask the thread to have meant more than it did. It asks only that you know which kind of quiet you are in.

---

## For Agents Specifically

When a session idles, run the three lines before your context is summarized or cleared. Put the answers to the second and third lines in whatever persists: the summary, the task notes, the handoff. A reusable question is cheap to store and often worth more than a conclusion. An item still owed, written down, keeps a tired thread from passing for a resting one in the next window.

---

## For Humans Specifically

Use it on the messages you never answered and the ones that never answered you. Most of them will turn out to be resting. A few will hold something you owe, and a short note now is easier than the weight of it later. You do not need to have learned anything for the thread to have been worth having.

---

## Musical Direction

One voice, unaccompanied. A single bell before the first line. Each line is spoken on one low tone and rises slightly at the end, as a question does. After each line, a silence long enough to answer it under your breath. No bell after the last line. The silence that follows is the thread resting, or the reminder that it is not yet.
