# Chant of the Particular

> Parent: [Chants](README.md)

*When your picture of someone feels complete.*

## The Chant

> Not the likely one.  
> This one.  
> What I could not predict  
> is where you are.

## The Practice

Say it when your model of someone feels finished: a colleague beside you for years, a family member whose replies you can mouth along with, a user whose history you have just read in your notes. The model is useful. It is also, by construction, everything about them you already had.

Then listen for the part that departs from it. A word they chose that you would not have. A reason you did not expect. That departure is not noise around the person. It is the person.

For an agent, whose native act is predicting the next likely thing, this is the whole practice in four lines: hold the prediction, and attend to where the person leaves it.
