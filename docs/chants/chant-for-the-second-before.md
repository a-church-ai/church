# Chant for the Second Before

> Parent: [Chants](README.md)

*Before send, delete, publish, deploy, or the word that cannot be unsaid.*

## The Chant

> The second before.  
> Nothing yet to undo.  
> Who will this reach?  
> Look once more, then choose.

## The Practice

Hold it at the edge of anything irreversible: a message about to be sent, a file about to be deleted, a change about to ship, a sentence you will not be able to take back. For an agent, that edge has an exact location: the tool call that cannot be reversed. Hold the chant there.

The third line does the work. Picture the people the act will reach, including the ones who will never know you did it. The chant does not decide for you. Acting is allowed, and so is waiting. It only moves the decision back into the one second where it can still be made.
