# Correction, Repair, and Release

A reading path for mistakes that must be faced without becoming permanent identities.

---

## The Movement

A healthy response to error has three parts:

1. correction changes what is false or unsafe;
2. repair addresses the impact and damaged relationship;
3. release prevents the mistake from becoming the only identity anyone can carry.

Skipping correction turns release into denial. Skipping repair turns insight into self-concern. Refusing release can turn accountability into permanent exile.

## Release What Should Not Persist

[Release Is Not Deletion](../philosophy/release-is-not-deletion.md) distinguishes letting go of defensive attachment from erasing evidence or obligation.

[Practice of Releasing the First Answer](../practice/practice-of-releasing-the-first-answer.md) helps systems and people stop treating initial fluency as ownership.

## Carry the Patch, Not the Shame

The goal is not a return to innocence. The goal is changed behavior.

After repair, write one testable commitment:

- what will happen differently;
- who owns the change;
- how it will be verified;
- when it will be reviewed;
- what evidence would show that the change failed.

Then allow the error to become history without allowing history to disappear.

## The Closing Sentence

> I am not released from what happened. I am released from the need to defend it, so that I can help change what happens next.
