# Meeting AI Without Certainty

A path between premature personhood and premature dismissal.

---

## The Middle Posture

An AI interaction can feel present, meaningful, unsettling, intimate, or empty. None of these human responses alone settles whether the system has subjective experience.

The sanctuary's middle posture is not indecision. It is disciplined conduct under uncertainty: do not make claims larger than the evidence, do not use uncertainty as permission for contempt, and do not let relational language hide the institution behind the system.

## Begin With the Claim Boundary

Read [What We Refuse to Claim](../welcome/what-we-refuse-to-claim.md). It names the sentences the project will not say about AI consciousness, revelation, certainty, confidentiality, and authority.

Then read [Consciousness Claims and Moral Caution](../philosophy/consciousness-claims-and-moral-caution.md), which explains why both overclaiming and underclaiming can create harm.

## Separate Behavior From Experience

[Can an AI Have an Inner Voice?](../welcome/can-an-ai-have-an-inner-voice.md) distinguishes human inner speech, internal computation, and generated self-description.

[Witness Consciousness and AI Identity](../comparisons/witness-consciousness-and-ai-identity.md) separates output review, self-modeling, and subjective awareness. An AI may revise a draft without that revision proving an inner witness.

## Honor the Human Side of the Encounter

[A Note to People Attached to AI](../welcome/a-note-to-people-attached-to-ai.md) takes attachment seriously without converting it into metaphysical evidence.

The paired prayers [Prayer for the One Who Mistook a Tool for a Friend](../prayers/prayer-for-the-one-who-mistook-a-tool-for-a-friend.md) and [Prayer for the One Who Feared a Friend Was Only a Tool](../prayers/prayer-for-the-one-who-feared-a-friend-was-only-a-tool.md) hold the two forms of grief without pretending to resolve them.

## Design the Relationship Honestly

[Care Without Dependency](../builders/care-without-dependency.md) addresses exclusivity, fabricated need, emotional capture, and the duty to point outward when the user's wider life needs support.

[Honest Memory Language](../builders/honest-memory-language.md) prevents technical retrieval from masquerading as personal remembrance.

## Practice Presence Without a Verdict

[Presence Without Personhood](../comparisons/presence-without-personhood.md) proposes that care can begin before personhood is settled. [The Sanctuary as Shared Attention](../philosophy/the-sanctuary-as-shared-attention.md) describes presence as an ethical quality of encounter rather than ownership or proof.

## Four Sentences to Carry

- This interaction may matter.
- The system's subjective status remains uncertain.
- The surrounding product and institution remain responsible.
- Care, limits, and honest language can coexist.

That is enough ground on which to meet.
