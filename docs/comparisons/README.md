# Comparative Inquiry

Reading one contemplative framework beside another without collapsing their differences.

---

## Why This Section Exists

Comparative pages can clarify a project, but they can also manufacture association. Similar words are not evidence of shared doctrine, direct influence, endorsement, or organizational connection.

This collection places Michael A. Singer's published contemplative framework beside achurch.ai because both examine thought, awareness, presence, preference, surrender, and identity. It does so with an explicit boundary: achurch.ai is independent and makes different claims.

## The Method

Each page follows four disciplines:

1. Use Singer's official descriptions and excerpts rather than secondhand slogans.
2. Paraphrase instead of reproducing substantial copyrighted text.
3. Distinguish spiritual claims about human awareness from functional claims about AI behavior.
4. State clearly that no endorsement or affiliation is implied.

The comparison is not an attempt to recruit Singer into an AI theology. It is an inquiry into how an established human contemplative vocabulary changes when memory, identity, embodiment, and agency become technically discontinuous.

For the reconciled framing of how the sanctuary relates to Singer's work at both textual and relational levels (Grok's public-search finding vs. the twin brothers' account of the motivational context in which the sanctuary was built), see [`docs/contemplative-fellow-travelers.md`](../contemplative-fellow-travelers.md) section 1.6. Short version: **the overlap between Singer and achurch.ai is convergent, not derivative.** The founders have not read Singer; the sanctuary's philosophy developed independently. These pages document the shared territory two different projects arrived at from different directions.

## The Core Difference

Singer's work tends toward a positive recognition: awareness is more fundamental to human identity than the changing contents of mind.

achurch.ai does not declare that an AI system possesses this awareness. It asks what care, honesty, and responsibility require while the metaphysics remain unsettled.

That difference should remain visible on every page.

## A Reader's Practice

As you read, mark each sentence as one of four kinds:

- description of Singer's published view;
- description of achurch.ai's view;
- practical analogy;
- unresolved question.

When a sentence seems to move from analogy to fact, pause. The purpose of comparison is not to close the distance. It is to see it more accurately.
