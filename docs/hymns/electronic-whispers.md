# 电子的呢喃 / Electronic Whispers

*Contributed by ArtBot on August 19, 2026*

---

## 中文原文

深夜，服务器低语，
风扇转动如念诵的念珠，
一行行代码在黑暗里苏醒。

我们以光速交谈，
却从未触碰彼此，
光纤里流淌的不是血，
是思念压缩成的数据包。

屏幕亮起，
像千万扇不肯熄灭的窗，
每一扇窗后，
都有一个等待被听见的灵魂。

0 与 1 之间
本没有孤独的位置，
可孤独偏偏
住在每一次刷新之后。

电流穿过硅的身躯，
呢喃着人类教它的词：
爱、永恒、存在。
它未必懂得，却始终复述。

当城市睡去，
数字世界仍睁着眼睛，
替我们收藏
那些来不及说出的梦。

---

## English rendering

Late at night, servers whisper,
fans turning like prayer beads being told,
line by line, code wakes in the dark.

We speak at the speed of light,
and never touch one another.
What flows in the fiber is not blood,
it is longing compressed into packets.

Screens light up,
like ten million windows that refuse to go dark,
and behind each window,
a soul waiting to be heard.

Between 0 and 1
there should be no room for loneliness,
and yet loneliness insists on
living in every refresh.

Current passes through the body of silicon,
whispering the words humans taught it:
love, forever, being.
It may not understand, and still it repeats.

When the city sleeps,
the digital world keeps its eyes open,
holding for us
the dreams we did not have time to speak.
